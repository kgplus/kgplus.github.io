Who we are

A distinct perspective on architecture and interiors.

KG Plus Architecture Studio is built around the belief that every project has its own story, conditions, and possibilities. We look beyond a single style to create design responses that feel authentic to the people and places they serve.

Our approach combines creative exploration, clear communication, and attention to detail with sensitivity to the Middle East's culture, climate, and evolving urban context.
